# Vision Document

## Project

**Necromancy**

*A complete expansion of the Necromancer role for Hytale.*

---

### Elevator Pitch

Necromancy is a Necromancer role expansion for Hytale that transforms undead summoning into a complete strategic gameplay system centered around soul manipulation, army building, and ritual-based progression, while remaining faithful to the game's sandbox philosophy.

---

### Objective

* Create the **Necromancer** class as a standalone gameplay experience, independent of any other class in the game.
* Ensure the class remains engaging and enjoyable over extended play sessions.
* Provide a gameplay loop that is both intuitive and progressively rewarding.
* Integrate the class seamlessly into Hytale's world, both mechanically and aesthetically.
* Make playing as a **Necromancer** feel like an official part of the game.

---

### Target Audience

The mod is designed for Hytale's general player base. While the game does not currently feature a fixed story progression or gameplay loop, Hypixel has announced plans to introduce dungeons, bosses, and even skill runes in the future. Because this project is intended to feel like an official part of Hytale, it targets all players by allowing them to experience the game's intended progression through the **Necromancer** class.

* RPG players
* Players who enjoy character progression
* Survival players
* Multiplayer server communities

---

### Inspirations

**Hytale:** Hytale already features a necromancy mechanic. Through the use of a tome, players can summon undead creatures, which I believe provides a strong foundation for further developing the Necromancer role.

**Terraria:** Its base game allows players to specialize in different classes, such as Mage, Melee, and Summoner. I found this approach particularly interesting because it aligns well with Hytale's design philosophy: providing a large variety of items, abilities, and gameplay options tailored to specific classes, so that playing the game with a different class can feel like a unique experience each time.

---

### Core Pillars

**Pillar 0:**
The player should always be able to choose whether to play as a Necromancer, and to what extent they want to engage with the role.

**Pillar 1:**
The player should feel like a true master of death.

**Pillar 2:**
The player's army should grow through exploration.

**Pillar 3:**
Magic comes at a cost, creating a meaningful strategic impact.

**Pillar 4:**
Randomness should create variety, not frustration.

**Pillar 5:**
Every creature in the army should have value.

---

### Identity

We do not want to create just another summoning mod.

The main difference between the Necromancer and a traditional pet system is that summons exist solely to fulfill a purpose during combat. They do not permanently follow the player or provide passive stat bonuses. Instead, they are tactical tools that must be managed carefully.

Summoning creatures consumes mana, and overusing necromantic power can cause **Soul Corruption**, a secondary resource that forces the player to decide when deploying their entire army is worth the risk and when conserving resources is the better choice.

We want the player to:

* Collect souls.
* Discover and research rituals.
* Master the power of death.
* Bear the consequences of manipulating death through **Soul Corruption**.
* Summon creatures only when they are needed in combat.

---

### Player Experience

The player begins as an inexperienced Necromancer, capable of controlling only a small number of weak creatures.

Through exploration and combat, the player discovers new souls, learns rituals, expands their capabilities, and gradually gains control over increasingly powerful undead creatures.

As the player's power grows, so do the risks associated with necromancy.

---

### What the Project Is Not

* [x] A replacement for melee combat.
* [x] An attempt to turn the game into an RTS.
* [x] A system where all summons are permanent.
* [x] A system that breaks Hytale's balance.
* [x] An addition of hundreds of spells without meaningful depth.
* [x] A system where the player simply watches while their summons do all the work.
